Whole vibe dictionary

Gen Z slang cheat code.

Use this like seasoning, not the whole bottle. A little slang makes it feel current; too much sounds like a fake TikTok comment section.

No capNo lie, seriously."I am sorry, no cap."
FrFor real."I hear you, fr."
BetOkay, understood."Bet, I will give you space."
ValidYour feelings make sense."Your anger is valid."
ReceiptsProof through actions."I need to bring receipts."
Locked inFocused and serious."I am locked in on changing."
FumbledMessed up badly."I fumbled how I treated you."
Side eyeSuspicious or annoyed."I get why you are side-eyeing me."
TeaThe truth or situation."The tea is I handled it wrong."
LowkeyA little, quietly."Lowkey, I should have listened sooner."
HighkeyVery obviously."Highkey, I was wrong."
Not itWrong, bad, unacceptable."My reaction was not it."
Say lessI understand and will act."Say less, I will stop pushing."
Vibe checkTesting the mood."This is my accountability vibe check."
Big yikesVery bad or awkward."How I acted was big yikes."
Real talkHonest conversation."Real talk, I hurt you."
SlayDoing great."You slay, even when you are mad."
AteDid something perfectly."You ate me up with the truth."
Left no crumbsCompletely proved a point."You left no crumbs explaining why it hurt."
PeriodEnd of discussion."Your feelings matter, period."
Glow upImprove visibly."My behavior needs a glow up."
Main characterConfident central energy."You deserve main character treatment."
DeluluDelusional."It was delulu to think sorry alone fixes it."
Green flagGood sign."Listening properly is the green flag."
Red flagBad sign."Ignoring your feelings was a red flag."
IckSomething unattractive or off-putting."Defending myself too fast was an ick."
GhostDisappear without replying."I will not ghost the hard conversation."
Soft launchSubtle reveal."This is not a soft launch apology."
Hard launchFull public reveal."I am hard launching accountability."
Love bombOverdo affection to avoid repair."I am not trying to love bomb you."
Touch grassGet grounded."I needed to touch grass and think."
Rent freeStuck in your head."What I did should not live rent free in your heart."
Pop offSpeak strongly or shine."You can pop off if you need to."
CookedIn trouble or done."I knew I was cooked when I hurt you."
RizzCharm."No rizz can replace accountability."
MidAverage, not enough."A mid apology is not enough."
Go offSay what you need to say."Go off. I will listen."
EnergyAttitude or vibe."I am coming with calmer energy."
Plot twistUnexpected change."Plot twist: I am actually listening."
EraCurrent phase."I am entering my do-better era."

Best combo

Slang + sincerity.

"I fumbled, no cap. Your feelings are valid, and I am not going to argue with them. I will bring receipts through my actions."

Do not overdo it

Too much slang gets cringe.

If every sentence has slang, it stops sounding real. Use one or two words, then speak plainly and respectfully.
